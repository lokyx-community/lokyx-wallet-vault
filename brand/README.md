# The LOKYX mark

The Ring: one thick stroke, open at the top right, with a single point set into the opening.

- Geometry (100 × 100): arc of radius 34 around (50, 50) from 12 o'clock round to 3 o'clock the long way, stroke 14, round ends; point of radius 7.5 at (74, 26).
- Below 24 px use stroke 16 and point 8.5; below 18 px, stroke 18 and point 9.
- One colour only: black on light, white (#F5F5F7) on dark. No gradients, outlines or shadows.
- Clear space: a quarter of the mark's width on every side.
- App icons: white mark at 52% of the tile on black (#000000).
- Next to the name: `LOKYX` in Geist SemiBold, letter-spacing 0.22em, the mark at the cap height plus a third.
